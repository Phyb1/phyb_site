Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a website in Zimbabwe?

PHYB packages start from $25/year for a Signpost — a 2-page site with domain and hosting included, no hidden fees. Full multi-page websites start from $80, one-time, with $25/year for domain and hosting from year two.

Can you build a website for Mvurwi, Bindura, or Harare businesses?

Yes — PHYB works with businesses across Zimbabwe. Most work is done remotely, so location isn't a limitation.

What's the difference between a Facebook page and a website?

A Facebook page can be suspended or banned and was never really yours to begin with. A website you own outright. Google and AI search tools also draw their answers from indexed websites, not Facebook pages, so a website gives you a real chance of being found that a Facebook-only presence doesn't.

Do you do e-commerce and bookings?

Yes, on the Premium package — online payments, a booking system, and WhatsApp API integration. Smaller packages include a WhatsApp ordering button, which covers most small businesses' needs without full e-commerce.

How long does it take to build my site?

Signpost: 24–48 hours. Basic: 5–7 days. Standard: 10–12 days. Premium: 15–20 days. You'll see a draft before launch either way.

Will my site appear on Google?

Every PHYB site is mobile-friendly and built with SEO basics in place from the start, which gives Google what it needs to index and rank the site. Ranking well for competitive terms still takes ongoing content and time, the same as with any website.

How do I pay?

Two options: pay directly by Ecocash transfer to the business's registered EcoCash number (confirmed manually once received), or pay instantly through Paynow by Ecocash, OneMoney, or card. Both are available on the payment page after your order is placed.
